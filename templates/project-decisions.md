# Decisions — <project name>

*What this is for: the decisions that stop a wrong turn later, with the road not taken. One of the
three files every project and area keeps, beside `glossary.md` and `terms.md`.*

*`/projects:new` and `/ongoing:new` copy this file to the folder as `decisions.md`. Delete this italic
block and the example entry once the first real decision is in.*

*Scope: decisions belonging to **this** project — architecture, tooling, framing, anything a later
session would otherwise re-derive or re-litigate. A decision that still means something with every
reference to this project, its stack and its stakeholders stripped out belongs in the cross-project
log instead. When it is unclear, it goes here — promotion is cheap, demotion is not.*

*Newest last. Entries are corrected by appending a later entry, not by rewriting an earlier one — the
record of what was believed in March is itself information. The documentation register applies: calm
declarative voice, success-mode framing, no invented urgency.*

---

## [YYYY-MM-DD] Decision title

**Context**: What made this a question.

**Decision**: What was decided.

**Rationale**: Why — including the evidence, and its date if it will age.

**Alternatives considered**: What was rejected, and why. This is the part a later session cannot
reconstruct: the decision is visible in the work, the road not taken is visible nowhere. It is what
stops the same option being re-proposed with enthusiasm next quarter.

**Generalises as**: *(optional)* The lesson with the project, the stack and the stakeholder stripped
out. An entry that grows one of these is a candidate for promotion to the cross-project log.

**Status**: Accepted / Superseded (by the YYYY-MM-DD entry) / Under review
